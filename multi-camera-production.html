Case Study

Multi-Camera Production & Direction

Directed an eight-minute remote camera production in a live television simulation, created for my Multi-Camera Production class at Asbury University.

Watch the Production Back to Portfolio
Context

Multi-Camera Production class at Asbury University

My Role

Writer, producer, and director, leading a crew of twelve

Format

Eight-minute live television simulation with three remote cameras

Audience

College students, ages 18 to 22

Overview

Directing in real time

I served as writer, producer, and director in a simulated real-time television environment, directing three remote cameras and leading a crew of twelve. In the video, you will hear my voice as I direct, along with my assistant director and other crew members, as we coordinate shots.

Why the video starts black: As the director, I call when the production goes "live," and the strict eight-minute timer begins. This simulates real-life television production.

Why the actors hold scripts: They volunteered for the project and were not required to memorize anything.

Audience

Who it's for

The target audience was college students, ages 18 to 22, male and female, from middle to upper-middle-class backgrounds, with high school-level and in-progress university-level education.

Directing the Production

What I ran from the control room

I directed three remote cameras, graphics, lighting, replay (recording), and audio. I led with clear and precise directions, demonstrating professional directing language.

Skills Demonstrated

  • Directing
  • Producing
  • Scriptwriting
  • Multi-Camera Production
  • Live Television
  • Crew Leadership
