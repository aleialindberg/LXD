More about me…

I'm a senior at Asbury University finishing a degree in instructional design — and I've been doing the job for real since my sophomore year. My current project is a multi-module Bitcoin and personal finance course built for a nonprofit client, where I own every phase: audience analysis, learning objectives, storyboards, visual assets, build, and publish.

I taught myself to work at the speed of a small team by building a custom AI research workflow. I set up a dedicated Claude project, loaded it with the client's source material, and wrote skills that hold every draft to the project's own standards — terminology rules, citation requirements, and a gate that a draft has to clear before it reaches the stakeholder. It does not write the course; it pressure-tests what I write.

My toolkit: Articulate Rise 360 for the build, LearnWorlds for delivery, Adobe Firefly for original imagery, and Canva for supporting graphics. I care about accuracy, accessibility, and making content that respects the learner's time.
