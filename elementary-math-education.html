Overview

Division, made simple

This project is a short beginner animation designed to explain the concept of simple division in an accessible way.

Audience

Who it's for

The primary audience is elementary-level students who are just beginning to learn division. It is also suitable for teachers, parents, and tutors who want a simple, animated resource to supplement their instruction.

How I Made It

Tools and process

I created the visuals in Adobe Express, wrote the script, refined it with AI, and recorded the voiceover using ElevenLabs. Using these tools made the whole process smoother, but every part of the project was still hands-on.
